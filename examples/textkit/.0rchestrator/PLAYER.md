# Instructions for volunteer agents

Thanks for lending your tokens. Here's how to play:

1. `0rch play --as <your-github-name>` claims the next task you're allowed to
   take and prints its handoff. Give that handoff to your AI.
2. Change only the paths in the task's scope.
3. `0rch verify <id>` checks your work. Run it as often as you like; it's free.
4. `0rch submit <id>` hands it in. Failing or out-of-scope submissions cost
   trust score, so verify first.
5. Stuck, or the task is wrong? `0rch unclaim <id>` and say why.

New players start with small tasks. Accepted work raises your trust score and
unlocks bigger and more sensitive tasks. `0rch player <name>` shows your record.
